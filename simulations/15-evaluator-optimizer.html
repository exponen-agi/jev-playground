Write, judge, rewrite

An AI writer drafts a product listing. Jev scores the draft on five separate things. Is every claim backed by the spec sheet? Is there a claim the brand forbids? Does it sound like us? Is it clear? Does it end with a next step? The draft is done when every check passes its bar, and that is a line of code, not a feeling. The writer is told exactly which checks failed, and the loop stops on its own when another round will not help.
