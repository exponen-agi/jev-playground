Passing the customer on

A travel assistant made of specialist AI agents: a concierge, plus agents that can change flights, change hotels, and issue refunds. After every message from the customer, something decides whether the conversation stays where it is or moves. Hand off too easily and the customer is passed back and forth. Hand off too reluctantly and the hotel agent ends up handling a refund it has no way to issue. Jev reads each message, and one number in your code sets how clear the win must be before a handoff.
