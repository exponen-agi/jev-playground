The foreman

Four AI workers fix bugs together: a researcher, a coder, a tester and a reviewer. Something has to decide who works next, and in most frameworks that is another large model re-reading the whole conversation on every turn. Here Jev makes that choice in a quarter of a second. It also checks whether the last piece of work was any good, and notices when the team is going round in circles.
