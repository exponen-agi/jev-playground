Is the confidence number load-bearing? — last year’s ledger, re-run

Take a few hundred rows you already categorised by hand, run them through, and bucket the results by the confidence that came back. Accuracy climbing with confidence is what makes a floor meaningful. A flat table means the number is decoration and nothing should be filed automatically — which is a thing you can find out in an afternoon, before you trust it with anything.
